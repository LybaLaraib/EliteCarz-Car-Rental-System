Policies

Privacy, terms, and refunds — all in one place.

Privacy Policy

We collect only what’s needed to operate EliteCarz: account details, booking information, and payment records. We do not sell personal data. Access is limited to authorized staff for support and operations.

  • Data used for bookings, support, and security.
  • Basic logs may be stored for fraud prevention and auditing.
  • You can request account data correction by contacting support.

Terms & Conditions

By using EliteCarz, you agree to provide accurate information, follow booking rules, and respect pickup/return timings. Admin actions are logged for safety and integrity of operations.

  • Bookings may auto-cancel if not completed within the stated window.
  • Availability depends on date overlap and confirmation status.
  • Misuse, fraud, or abusive behavior may result in account restriction.

Refund Policy

Refunds depend on booking status and timing. If a booking is cancelled by the system or marked cancelled by support, the related payment record will be reviewed.

  • Pending payments generally do not require refunds.
  • Paid bookings may be eligible based on cancellation reason and policy window.
  • For disputes, contact support with your booking ID.
